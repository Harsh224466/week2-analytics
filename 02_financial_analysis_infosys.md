# Financial Statement Analysis — Infosys Ltd. (NSE: INFY)

## 1. Company Overview
Infosys Limited is a leading Indian multinational IT services and consulting company listed on both the NSE and BSE, and a core constituent of the Nifty 50 and Sensex indices.

## 2. FY 2025–26 Consolidated Financial Snapshot
| Financial Metric | FY 2025–26 Value | YoY Performance / Context |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Consolidated Revenue** | ₹1,78,650 Cr | +9.6% YoY growth |
| **Net Profit (PAT)** | ₹29,474 Cr | Strong bottom-line expansion |
| **Basic EPS** | ₹71.58 | +11.0% YoY growth |
| **Total Assets** | ₹1,55,967 Cr | Up from ₹1,48,903 Cr in FY25 |
| **Shareholders' Equity** | ₹92,852 Cr | Strong net-worth base |
| **Cash & Investments** | ₹43,075 Cr | High balance-sheet liquidity |
| **Free Cash Flow (FCF)** | ₹33,097 Cr | 112.3% of Net Profit |
| **Dividend Per Share (DPS)** | ₹48.00 | +11.6% YoY increase |

## 3. Financial Statement & Ratio Interpretation
* **Profitability (P&L Statement):** Adjusted operating margin stood at **21.0%**, and Net Profit Margin reached **16.5%** (`29,474 / 1,78,650`), supported by cost discipline and $14.9B in large deal Total Contract Value (TCV).
* **Capital Efficiency (Balance Sheet + P&L):** Return on Equity (ROE) was **31.6%**, demonstrating strong profit generation per rupee of shareholder equity with minimal debt burden.
* **Cash Generation (Cash Flow Statement):** Free Cash Flow conversion exceeded 100% of net profit (**112.3%**), enabling Infosys to fund a ₹18,000 Cr share buyback and ₹48/share dividend internally without borrowing.
* **Analyst Conclusion:** Infosys operates an asset-light, high-margin, cash-generative business model with strong liquidity and consistent shareholder returns.
