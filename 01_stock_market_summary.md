# Stock Market Fundamentals — Summary

## 1. Core Market Concepts & Exchanges
* **Stock Market:** A regulated marketplace where buyers and sellers trade shares of publicly listed companies, enabling businesses to raise capital and investors to build wealth.
* **NSE & BSE:** India’s two primary stock exchanges. **BSE** (Bombay Stock Exchange, est. 1875) is Asia's oldest exchange, while **NSE** (National Stock Exchange, est. 1992) introduced electronic screen-based trading and leads in daily equity and derivatives volume.
* **Nifty 50 & Sensex:** Benchmark indices tracking overall market sentiment. **Nifty 50** tracks the top 50 large-cap companies on the NSE; **Sensex** tracks 30 established companies on the BSE.
* **IPO & SME IPO:** An **Initial Public Offering (IPO)** is when a private company first sells shares to the public on the mainboard. An **SME IPO** allows Small and Medium Enterprises to list on dedicated platforms (NSE Emerge, BSE SME) with relaxed listing norms.

## 2. Valuation Metrics & Corporate Actions
| Metric / Term | Definition & Significance |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Market Capitalization** | Total market value of a company's outstanding shares (`Share Price × Total Shares`), grouped into Large-Cap, Mid-Cap, and Small-Cap. |
| **EPS (Earnings Per Share)** | Net profit allocated per equity share: `(Net Profit - Preference Dividends) / Total Outstanding Shares`. |
| **P/E Ratio** | Valuation multiple (`Market Price per Share / EPS`) showing how much investors pay per ₹1 of earnings. |
| **P/B Ratio** | Compares market price to book value (`Market Price per Share / Book Value per Share`); useful for banking and asset-heavy companies. |
| **Dividend** | Portion of net profit distributed to shareholders in cash, typically declared per share. |
| **Bonus & Stock Split** | **Bonus Issue:** Free extra shares issued from reserves (e.g., 1:1). **Stock Split:** Divides existing shares into smaller face-value units (e.g., ₹10 face value split into five ₹2 shares) to boost liquidity. Neither changes total investment value. |
| **Trading Volume** | Total number of shares bought and sold during a session; indicates liquidity and price-move strength. |

## 3. Company Financial Statements & Key Ratios
* **Balance Sheet:** Snapshot of financial position on a specific date (`Assets = Liabilities + Shareholders' Equity`).
* **Profit & Loss (P&L) Statement:** Summarizes revenues, operating expenses, taxes, and net profit over a quarter or financial year.
* **Cash Flow Statement:** Tracks actual cash inflows and outflows across **Operating**, **Investing**, and **Financing** activities.
* **Basic Financial Ratios:**
  * **Return on Equity (ROE):** `Net Profit / Shareholders' Equity` — measures return generated on shareholder capital.
  * **Operating Margin:** `Operating Profit (EBIT) / Revenue` — measures core operational profitability.
  * **Debt-to-Equity (D/E):** `Total Debt / Shareholders' Equity` — measures financial leverage and solvency risk.
  * **Current Ratio:** `Current Assets / Current Liabilities` — measures short-term liquidity.
